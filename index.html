Xiaomi 17T

Number One Premium Mid-Range Phone in Nepal

Overview

In the number one position for the best premium mid-range phone in Nepal right now is the Xiaomi 17T. It is priced cheaper than the Vivo V70, while still offering a pocketable form factor just like the Vivo V70. But the main reason the Xiaomi 17T takes the number one spot is because of its excellent performance.

You are getting the Dimensity 8500 Ultimate chip here, which is significantly better than what you get on phones like the Vivo V70, Galaxy A57, and Nothing Phone 4a Pro. So even after a few years, the Xiaomi 17T should still feel faster and more reliable.

Camera Performance

Likewise, the cameras on the Xiaomi 17T are also extraordinary. It performs exceptionally well, and you also get a 5x telephoto zoom lens. Usually, in this price range, we only see 3x or 3.5x telephoto cameras, but here Xiaomi has given a proper 5x lens. And it captures amazing portraits, especially 5x portraits, along with some excellent macro shots as well.

Because of the Leica optimization, the photos also have a very distinct character. You can take beautiful Leica-style portraits, including amazing black-and-white portraits. So overall, this is arguably one of the best camera phones in this price range.

Display, Battery & Software

After performance and cameras, the display experience is also really good. It has a 12-bit AMOLED display, whereas most phones at this price usually offer 8-bit or 10-bit panels. Battery life is also amazing, thanks to the large 6500mAh silicon-carbon battery. And to top it all off, the phone runs on Android 16, with four years of OS updates as well.

So overall, the Xiaomi 17T feels like a very well-balanced phone. Whether it is performance, cameras, display, battery, or software, everything here comes together really nicely, making it an excellent option if you are thinking of buying a premium mid-range phone in Nepal in 2026.
